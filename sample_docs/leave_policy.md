# Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per year, accrued at 2 days per month. Up to 10 unused days may be carried over to the next year.

Sick leave is 12 days per year and requires a medical certificate for absences longer than 2 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver.
