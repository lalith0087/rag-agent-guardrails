# Expense Policy

Employees may claim up to 1,500 rupees per day for meals while travelling on company business. Receipts are required for any claim above 300 rupees.

Domestic flights must be booked in economy class at least 7 days before departure. Hotel stays are capped at 6,000 rupees per night in metro cities and 4,000 rupees per night elsewhere.

Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of the trip. Late claims need written approval from the department head.
